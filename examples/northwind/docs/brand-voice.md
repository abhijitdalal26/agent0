# Northwind Health brand voice (fictional, demo only)

- Warm, plain, confident. Short sentences. Talk to one person ("you").
- Lead with convenience and care, never with fear, shame or body image.
- Never promise outcomes. Describe the process and the support instead.
- Avoid medical jargon; say "your clinician" not "the provider".
- Calls to action: "Start your assessment", "See if you're eligible".
