# Northwind Health — approved product facts (fictional, demo only)

## Northwind Weight Care program

- A licensed Northwind Health clinician reviews every assessment before any prescription.
- If prescribed, medication ships in 1 to 2 business days with free tracked delivery.
- Monthly program price: $149, cancel anytime, no membership fee.
- Patients get unlimited messaging with the care team during treatment.
- Treatment is not available in all states.

## Northwind Oral Program
- An oral (non-injection) option is available for eligible patients.
- Eligibility is decided by the clinician after the assessment.
- Monthly price for the oral option: $199.
